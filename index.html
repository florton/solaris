solaris — a standalone 4D artwork.

      
You type a thought. A small language model (MiniLM-L6, 22.7M parameters, q8) reads it — once, in a single forward pass, entirely on your own GPU. The sculpture is the geometry of what the model holds together: at each of its 7 layers, every token's hidden state is compared with every other (cosine similarity), and the association graph is embedded into space so that tokens separated by bottlenecks land far apart. The body is the smooth surface fused from those points; the filaments within are the association graph itself. Layers stack into a fourth axis you can fly through, rotationally aligned so the same word traces one continuous path. Early layers hold surface associations; late layers compress toward abstraction. That re-organizing is the exhibit.

      
This is not attention — it is association geometry, and the piece says so. It is also not a chatbot: the model never writes a word back.

      
Same thought, same weights, same sculpture — forever. No sampling anywhere. Nothing you type ever leaves this machine; the weights ship with the page and after the first load it works offline.
